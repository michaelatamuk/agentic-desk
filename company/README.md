# Company products and platforms

Products built by other teams in the company. They share the platform, the customers or the
technology, so we track them. We do not own their roadmap, and nothing here is a commitment from our
team.

One distinction matters: openJiuwen is a company platform we **contribute to**, but do not own. This
folder holds its product context; our issues, designs and release notes are in
`../projects/openjiuwen/`.

| Folder | Product | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| `openjiuwen/` | openJiuwen | Platform context, code studies and release notes; our contribution work is in `../projects/openjiuwen/` |
| `ppma/` | PPMA | A memory-first manager for long-running projects |
| `digitalmate/` | DigitalMate | A HarmonyOS task app: task screens, bot chat and the product roadmap |
| `orbit/` | Orbit | The per-user cloud engine behind DigitalMate; today a harness, planned as a JiuwenSwarm plugin |
| `sciencediscovery/` | ScienceDiscovery | A local research workspace built on JiuwenSwarm |

Two boundaries keep this folder useful:

- **`projects/` is what we build and maintain.** LAIX, Cortex, Max and Channels live there, plus our
  openJiuwen contribution work.
- **`industry/` is external.** Other companies, competitors and public projects live there.

The point of the separation is the claim: a note in `projects/` means we are doing this; a note in
`industry/` means someone else is doing this; a note here means our company is doing it, but we do not
own it.
