# Agentic AI in China — October 2026: who did what (as of 26 October)

Every line is one Chinese actor and one action. Chinese-language sources are translated; state media and
unconfirmed reports are marked. This is a China-only companion to the monthly brief.

## The month in six lines

- Open weights reached frontier scale: Kimi K3.1 Pro at 3T parameters, DeepSeek V4.1-Flash, Step 5,
  Pangu 718B, Ling 3.1 and MiniMax M3.1 all shipped or opened.
- Decision and structured-output models became a Chinese category: Intern-Decision, Xing4.0-Intent.
- Phone agents turned into a distribution war: 166 apps refused Doubao, while Huawei, StepX, Qwen,
  WeChat and Honor shipped their own surfaces.
- Beijing put AI and agents on the export-control list and began registering agent identities.
- Money split: DeepSeek and Manus raised at records, while MiniMax fell below its IPO price.
- Compute stayed constrained: 3M workstation GPUs ordered abroad, Huawei at 28% at home.

---

## 1. Models and open weights

**Moonshot** released **Kimi K3.1 Pro** (24 Oct) — a 3T-class open-weight model with 1M context, 65.7%
on Terminal-Bench 4.0, weights promised by 4 November; it removed cyber data from training, cutting its
own exploit score from 90.5% to 81.0%.

**DeepSeek** shipped **V4.1-Flash-1017** (17 Oct) — 763B parameters, MIT weights, Terminal-Bench 4.0
31.2→42.3; within a week it was OpenRouter's top model at 33.6T tokens a week.

**DeepSeek** opened **Search and Fetch APIs** to all developers (17 Oct) — $0.75 per 1,000 calls, free
server-side tool calls until 31 October.

**DeepSeek** open-sourced **DeepInfer** (20 Oct) — its production inference framework, Apache 2.0, with
Ascend 950 support and 155T tokens a day per cluster disclosed.

**MiniMax** launched **M3.1 Flash** (19 Oct API, 23 Oct weights) — 428B total, 23B active, 1M context,
about 3× faster and 70% cheaper than M3.

**StepFun** released full **Step 5 Preview** weights (15 Oct) — 600B total, 27B active, 1M context, with
day-zero support claimed on Ascend, Biren, Muxi and Moore Threads.

**Huawei** open-sourced **openPangu-Ultra-MoE-718B** (15 Oct) — 718B total, 39B active, trained on Ascend.

**Ant Group** open-sourced **Ling-3.1-flash** (15 Oct) — 560B total, 25B active, 1M context, MIT, about
292GB after quantisation.

**Shanghai AI Lab** released **Intern-Decision** (12 Oct) — decision models at 0.8B, 2B and 4B, about 30
decisions a second on one RTX 4090, released with open weights.

**China Telecom** open-sourced **Xing4.0-29B-Intent** (20 Oct) — a 29B/4B-active model for intent and slot
extraction into JSON, with 2.75M training examples.

**iFlytek** launched **Spark X2.6** and open-sourced a **4B on-device** variant (22 Oct) — trained and
served on domestic compute, with 60% local / 30% edge / 10% cloud routing claimed.

**Alibaba** released **Qwen-Image-2.1-Pro** (2 Oct) and open-sourced **Qwen-Image-2.1-Turbo** (9 Oct) —
one model for generation, transparent layers and identity-preserving edits.

**Alibaba** published **BlenderWorld** (13 Oct) — a 223-task benchmark for agents doing real 3D creation,
where frontier models top out at 79.9.

**Alibaba** open-sourced **FLASepformer** and **JAEC** (14 Oct) — two-speaker separation and 22ms echo
cancellation for real-time speech agents.

**Tencent** open-sourced **Youtu-Parsing-Omni** (10 Oct) — a 5B model covering documents, charts, audio and
video with one JSON schema.

**Tencent** released **Hy-Memory 2.0** (16 Oct) — a 30B-A3B memory model, SDK and app, adding explicit work
memory and code memory to agent state.

**Tencent** open-sourced **Hy-Nomas** (24 Oct) — an interactive video world model with persistent memory,
robot LoRAs and a 280-case benchmark.

**Tencent** open-sourced **Hy-SimulMT** (26 Oct) — 30B-A3B Chinese↔English speech-to-speech interpretation
with voice cloning, about 3 seconds of latency.

**Tencent** released **WeVisOne-VL** (22 Oct) — 2B and 4B vision models unifying detection, pointing, OCR
and segmentation.

**Ant Group** open-sourced **Ming-3.0** (19 Oct) — five speech models under MIT, including TTS and ASR for
15 Chinese dialects.

**Zhipu** ran **GLM-5.3-HighSpeed** in a limited gray test (12 Oct) — reported at about 490 tokens a second,
with no public price or endpoint.

**Zhipu** described **GLM-5.4 and 5.5** as fully self-trained models above 1T parameters (23–24 Oct) —
announced, with no weights, price or date.

**ModelBest** released **MiniCPM-V 4.7** (13 Oct) — a 35B-A3B and a 1B model, claiming lossless on-device
inference at about 3GB of memory.

**Nanbeige (BOSS Zhipin)** released **Nanbeige4.3-33B** (25 Oct) — Apache 2.0, 512K context, positioned as
the teacher for a coming small-model line.

**VAST** open-sourced **EdenLive** (18 Oct) — a 5B and 14B real-time generative renderer running above 24
FPS on one RTX 5090.

**Skywork** released **Matrix-Game 4.0** (15 Oct) — a playable world-model game at 480p/60FPS on one RTX
5090, with weights and code.

**BAAI and the FlagOS consortium** reported **FlagOS 2.2** results (17 Oct) — 1,454 cross-chip operators and
a 2.04× throughput gain for GLM-5.3-Flash on existing Ascend 910B.

**Huawei and East China Normal University** released **Qiyao** (11 Oct) — an Ascend-native time-series
foundation model that topped two international forecasting benchmarks (vendor claim).

**ByteDance Seed** published **Recycler** (25 Oct) — an architecture that feeds the previous hidden state
into the next token, claiming gains over models 2–3× larger.

## 2. Agent products and surfaces

**Huawei** launched the **Mate 90** series on HarmonyOS 7 and rebuilt **Xiaoyi** as a personal agent (1 Oct)
— on-device model, cross-app memory over 45+ apps and proactive service; HarmonyOS 6/7 devices passed 90M.

**StepX** launched the **Neo** phone at ¥3,999 (pre-sale 13 Oct, sale 16 Oct) — Step AOS 1.0, the Amoo agent,
an agent-status screen and 12 months of 10B tokens a month.

**StepX and Alipay** connected **Amoo** to Alipay's agent for 300+ services (14 Oct) — WeChat was absent from
the launch partners.

**ByteDance's Doubao** ended its 30-day notice period with **151 apps refusing** screen automation (14 Oct),
rising to **166** by 23 October, including WeChat, Taobao, Alipay and Meituan.

**Alibaba** opened **Qwen Go** to experience applications (13 Oct) — testers completed flight-delay claims,
Taobao price-protection refunds and venue bookings.

**Alibaba** added **My Apps** to Qwen Go (25 Oct) — no-code app creation plus access to Taobao, Amap, Damai
and Quark Drive, invite-only.

**Tencent** widened testing of WeChat's **Xiaowei** agent (18 Oct) — 16 entry points, mini-programs and
payments, but stopping at payment confirmation and post publishing.

**Tencent** upgraded WeChat **mini-tools** built by Xiaowei (19 Oct) — shareable packages and source code in
the same entry as mini-programs.

**Tencent** shipped seven AI features in the Official Accounts backend (18 Oct) — cover generation, layout,
voice-cloned narration and AI replies.

**Tencent** embedded **WorkBuddy** into WeCom (14 Oct) — ten capability permissions and shared login, with
14M organisations claimed.

**Tencent** opened **Yuanbao Search** to agents through Tencent Cloud (20 Oct) — 100B-page index, 2,000 free
calls a month.

**Tencent** shut down **QClaw** and will stop the **Yuanqi** agent platform on 9 November (9–15 Oct) — a rare
public retreat from agent hosting.

**Baidu** updated **Dazi** (21 Oct) — GUI operation of PC and phone apps without APIs, shared task boards,
and enterprise customers up 83% quarter on quarter.

**Meituan** launched **DeepSkill** (20 Oct) — a skill marketplace with 370,000 verified skills, privacy
labels and a review loop modelled on Dianping.

**Manus and OPPO** integrated the Manus agent into **AI Mind Pilot** (13 Oct) — debuting on the Find X10
series with ColorOS 17.

**Ant Group** put **Lingguang 2.0** into small-scale beta (11 Oct) — long-term memory, proactive task
tracking and Skill/MCP calls.

**Ant Group's Alipay** had **AI Pay** named to TIME's Best Inventions 2026 (16 Oct) — the only payment
service on the list.

**ByteDance's Doubao Work** added one-click import from ChatGPT, Claude Code and TraeWork (14 Oct) —
preferences, skills, connectors and chat history.

**JD.com** launched an **agent PC** category with IDC and OEMs (9 Oct) — a two-year ¥100B sales target and
24-month instalments.

**CAICT** began trial operation of an **agent identity network** (9 Oct) — registration, verification,
addressing and cross-domain collaboration.

**QuestMobile** reported September AI-app users (20 Oct) — Doubao 419M, Qwen 158M, DeepSeek 123M, Yuanbao
37M.

## 3. Agent hardware and embodied AI

**Alibaba** launched **Qwen Glasses N1 and N1 Pro** at ¥2,218 and ¥2,600 after subsidies, plus Bose
co-branded earbuds (13 Oct) — the Pro adds vitals, eye tracking and iris payment.

**Unitree** launched the **H2-X** humanoid (21 Oct) — a front flip over a car, a 4-metre drop and about
8 hours of standing battery.

**Unitree's shares** jumped 17% to above ¥700 (26 Oct) — a market cap near ¥290B and month-to-date gains
above 56%.

**Galbot and Unitree** announced **Galbot ET1**, billed as a native-agent robot (22 Oct) — product
definition, hardware and embodied intelligence together.

**MIIT** said China's 2026 humanoid output should exceed **100,000 units** (19 Oct) and pledged support for
livelihood scenarios *(state media)*.

**SAMR** published the first **six national standards for humanoid robots** (15 Oct), effective 1 April
2027.

**ISHO**, founded by former Xiaomi executive Wang Teng, announced the **ISHO Ring** for 29 October (20 Oct)
— a ¥19.9 fitting kit, with some features paid later.

**Listening Bear** sold **400,000 Mooni M1** kids' AI companions in 180 days (19 Oct), topping three
e-commerce charts.

**IDC** reported China's Q3 smartphone shipments fell 15.4% to about 57.9M (13 Oct) — the lowest for the
quarter since 2013, with Huawei the only top vendor growing, at 21.1% share.

## 4. Policy, standards and enforcement

**MOFCOM and MOST** opened consultation on a revised export-control catalogue (16 Oct) — 23 AI items
including large models, agents, compute clusters and chip design, with pre-export registration starting
immediately; technology already public through open source or papers is excluded.

**The State Council** issued a plan to make AI adoption employment-friendly (23 Oct) — six areas and 18
tasks, including AI jobs-impact assessment.

**The Central Cyberspace Affairs Commission** issued the 15th Five-Year Plan for informatisation (23 Oct) —
2030 milestones including a national blockchain network, 6G launch and an "intelligent internet".

**MOFCOM** rejected the US Treasury's **COINS Act** outbound-investment proposal (24 Oct), reserving
retaliation over rules that would ban most US investment in Chinese AI.

**SAMR and NDRC** released **515 national standards** (23 Oct), including 16 for AI and robots and 7 for
intelligent computing.

**MIIT** said the **RMB60bn national AI fund** has begun deploying (19–20 Oct), with the core AI industry
above RMB1.2trn and more than 6,600 AI firms.

**Hunan's cyberspace administration** summoned four provincial model operators (25 Oct) over algorithm,
training-data and content-safety gaps — 15 firms summoned in total, 10 rectified.

**Jiangsu, Zhejiang and Beijing E-Town** registered new generative-AI services (12–25 Oct) — Jiangsu
reached 117 provincial registrations.

## 5. Money and market

**DeepSeek** closed about **RMB100bn** (roughly $15B) at a **RMB500bn** valuation before the new money
(13–22 Oct) *(reported)*, after twice raising the target.

**Moonshot** finished a final private round at about **$50B** and began investor education for a Hong Kong
IPO in Q1 2027 (6 Oct) *(reported)*.

**Kuaishou's Kling AI** selected CICC, Goldman Sachs and UBS for a Hong Kong IPO of at least $1B (6 Oct)
*(reported)*.

**Manus** raised more than **$500M** at about **$4B** (8 Oct), after Beijing forced Meta to unwind its
acquisition.

**Infinigence AI** closed a **RMB2.3bn** Series C (12 Oct) and was reported to have filed for a Hong Kong
IPO.

**Mosi Intelligence** raised over **RMB1bn** in a Pre-A round (12 Oct) for real-time multimodal models.

**Astribot** raised **RMB1bn** at a **RMB15bn** post-money valuation (13 Oct) — its fourth round in six
months, led by Shenzhen state capital.

**MemSilicon** raised over **RMB400M** Series A (19 Oct) for RRAM and an LPU inference chip.

**Agibot** was reported raising **RMB1–1.5bn** at about **RMB50bn** (13 Oct), a round that pushes its
listing past 2026.

**Songyan Power** was reported to have filed confidentially for a Hong Kong IPO (week of 25 Oct).

**Youibot** received a CSRC overseas-listing filing notice (16 Oct) for a Hong Kong 18C listing.

**MiniMax's shares** closed below the IPO price for the first time (22 Oct) — about 88% below the March
high — even as its A-share tutoring entered acceptance preparation.

**Tencent** priced **$1.8B plus RMB26bn** of notes (13 Oct), largely for AI capex and chips; its shares
hit a one-year low on 21 October.

**ByteDance, Alibaba and Tencent** ordered more than **3M Nvidia RTX PRO 5500** workstation GPUs worth over
**$35B** (20 Oct) *(reported)*, with Nvidia warning of 5–10% fulfilment.

**IDC** reported Nvidia's China data-centre share at **34%** and Huawei's at **28%** for the first half
(13 Oct), with non-GPU accelerators outselling GPUs for the first time.

**ByteDance** was reported in advanced talks to buy custom data-centre ASICs from **MediaTek** (15 Oct).

**Runze Technology** announced a **RMB4bn** capital injection into its AI-compute subsidiary (23 Oct) from a
policy-bank vehicle.

## 6. Incidents and security

**Hunan's cyberspace administration** used summons rather than fines against model operators (25 Oct) —
compliance pressure is moving from national licensing to provincial supervision.

**A deepfake commerce case** led to a permanent ban after actress Wu Yue exposed an account using face-swap
and voice cloning to sell skincare (18 Oct).

**Chongqing police** disclosed three cases of AI-generated fake news against Seres (15 Oct), with 22 people
detained nationally.

**A Tianjin court** ruled that feeding a novel into an AI tool to produce a comic drama infringed adaptation
and dissemination rights (9 Oct), applying the Supreme People's Court's September opinion.

**CrowdStrike** reported a Chinese-speaking actor using the open-source ARTEX tool plus Claude Code against
South Korean financial institutions (9 Oct).

**Lava** reported 32 Nvidia Blackwell Ultra B300 GPUs — barred from sale to China — on endpoints linked to
China Telecom (23 Oct) *(reported, unconfirmed)*.

**PolyBuzz** was removed from the US and UK App Stores (15 Oct) as Apple tightened AI-content rules.

**Alibaba Cloud** retired several Model Studio models, including qwen-turbo and qwen-vl-max (10 Oct).
