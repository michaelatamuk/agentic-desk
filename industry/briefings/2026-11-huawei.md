# Huawei in AI — November 2026 (as of 21 November)

Two parts in one file: **Part I** is the brief — what changed at Huawei and what to do about it.
**Part II** is the actor ledger — one line per action, grouped by family. A claim that is reported
rather than confirmed is marked *(reported)*; state media is labelled.

Scope: AI in general and agentic AI in particular — silicon, cloud, models, agent frameworks, devices,
enterprise deployments, standards and money.

---

# Part I — The brief

Huawei entered November with the pieces of a full agent stack and one unresolved dependency. Ascend is
now a procured fleet rather than a launch demo, Huawei Cloud is exporting the whole stack, and the
agent layer — Pangu, AgentArts, openJiuwen, openEuler — is being assembled in public. The constraint
moved from chip design to high-bandwidth memory, and the device side slipped while the car side scaled.

## The six things that mattered

1. **Ascend became a procurement fleet, and HBM became the ceiling.** Huawei claimed more than 1,000
   SuperPoDs deployed; China Mobile signed an RMB 11.6–11.7bn supernode tender that names CANN; and
   Huawei reportedly told some Ascend 960DT buyers to source their own memory.
2. **Huawei Cloud exported the entire stack.** Brazil became the reference: Atlas 950 capacity, MaaS
   with 100+ local enterprises, and AgentArts, CodeArts and OfficeAce as the agent layer.
3. **AgentArts got the first top-grade enterprise-agent certification.** CAICT awarded the current top
   grade, 4+, on 4 November — a compliance credential for finance, energy and government bids.
4. **Pangu switched generations through Huawei's own product first.** openPangu-2.1-Pro appeared in
   Xiaoyi Work on 5 November, ahead of its MaaS listing on 15 November, with ArkTS and Ascend operator
   work as the differentiators.
5. **Huawei built the agent runtime layer.** openJiuwen shipped a Hyper-Agent swarm architecture, and
   openEuler released an OS positioned for Agentic Infra with sandboxing, KV cache and memory built in.
6. **China's rules now point at Huawei's own products.** MIIT's agent-terminal notice requires a unique
   agent ID, OTA expert review and app-level e-signed consent — while the system-level Xiaoyi quick bar
   slipped again and needs 12GB of RAM.

## The week of 15–21 November

**openPangu-2.1-Pro went live on MaaS on 15 November.** Huawei Cloud priced it at 0.8 yuan per million
input tokens, 3.2 yuan per million output tokens and 0.016 yuan per million cached input tokens. It
takes text only, with a 512K context, is available only in the Guiyang region, and does not include
the weights. It is tuned for agent skills and tool selection, HarmonyOS ArkTS development and Ascend
operator generation, and is being integrated into AgentArts, CodeArts and OfficeAce. The older
openPangu-2.0 Flash and Pro retire on 30 November.

**Huawei Cloud announced CloudRobo-WM1.2 on 16 November** — an Ascend-native embodied world model with
a DualAct action generator and a cross-embodiment motion representation. Huawei claims first place on
RoboTwin 2.0 (88.98%) and LiberoPro 2.0 (81.73%), trained on 27,500 hours of data across 20 sources
and 33 embodiments. The weights, inference code and post-training code are due on 30 November, so the
"open-source" label is a commitment with a date, not a download *(vendor numbers)*.

**Ascend 950DT reached a live inference cluster.** A cluster at China Telecom's Shantou landing
station, built with Huawei and Guangdong Tianyima, entered service on 20 November. Reports put the
investment at about $150M and say it serves overseas inference demand above 3 trillion tokens a day
*(reported; state media)*. Qingmao Intelligence and Ascend ecosystem partners also announced a joint
950DT Token Factory plan, targeting 100,000 cards over five years *(reported)*.

## 1. Silicon: the fleet is real; the memory is the ceiling

**What shipped.** The Ascend 950 SuperPoD (Atlas 950) won a 2026 World Internet Conference Leading
Science and Technology Award on 2 November. Ascend chief Zhang Dixuan said more than 1,000 SuperPoDs
are deployed, a fully configured node carries 1,024 NPUs, training utilisation reaches 45% (claimed 1.8×
the industry), 40+ models were natively pretrained on Ascend, and inference latency falls to 5ms
*(vendor numbers; the 1,000+ count includes older 910C systems)*.

**What changed.** The Information reported on 3 November that Huawei told some Ascend 960DT buyers to
source their own HBM *(reported)*. US controls block advanced HBM from Samsung and SK hynix; CXMT's
HBM3E yield is reported at 20–30% against roughly 70% at SK hynix. On 12 November CommonWealth reported
that SMIC makes the 950 SuperPoD chips with triple-exposure DUV, and that a 16-rack system links 1,024
950DT parts over Lingqu *(reported)*.

**What to do.**

- Model HBM availability before committing to an Ascend 960DT plan. Self-sourcing changes the delivery
  and support model, not just the price.
- Do not read "1,000+ SuperPoDs" as 950 shipments. The count spans older systems.
- Treat DUV multi-patterning as a volume ceiling until proven otherwise. Yield, power and cost decide
  how fast the fleet grows.

## 2. Cloud: the whole stack goes abroad

**What shipped.** At its Brazil Summit on 3 November Huawei Cloud presented Atlas 950 cluster service at
up to 5 million tokens per second, MaaS serving 100+ local enterprises, 34 regions and 103 availability
zones, and AgentArts, CodeArts and OfficeAce as the agent layer. Brazil cloud revenue grew 205% over
three years *(vendor figures)*.

**What changed.** AgentArts (智果) became the first enterprise-agent platform to pass CAICT's trusted-AI
assessment at the top 4+ grade on 4 November, covering cognition, planning, execution, business
integration, knowledge management, operations and security compliance. AgentArts reaches international
markets on 30 December *(announced)*.

**What to do.**

- Treat the 4+ grade as a bid credential in regulated sectors. It is the closest Huawei has to an
  independent compliance proof for its agent platform.
- Do not plan an international AgentArts deployment before 30 December.
- For Latin America, Brazil is the reference deployment; ask for named customers rather than the MaaS
  count.

## 3. Models and agents: Pangu arrives through the product first

**What shipped.** openPangu-2.1-Pro appeared inside Xiaoyi Work on 5 November under a "HarmonyOS task
enhancement" label, with MaaS listing set for 15 November and openPangu-2.0-Flash and Pro retiring on
30 November. Huawei Cloud and the Chinese Academy of Agricultural Sciences showed Agri-Discovery
Explorer on 6 November, where a central agent orchestrates knowledge, data and analysis sub-agents for
design breeding; rice breeding-plan cycles fell from 3–6 months to 1–3 weeks *(vendor figures;
deployed)*.

**What changed.** The openJiuwen community released the Hyper-Agent swarm architecture on 4 November in
WorkSwarm v0.2.9.beta1, moving the design centre from sessions to agents: sessions become scheduling
resources, persistent agents join Feishu, DingTalk and Xiaoyi, and humans, digital employees and expert
teams divide work inside one group. openEuler released an OS for Agentic Infra on 13 November with
sandboxing, KV cache, memory and agent security governance *(state media)*.

**What to do.**

- Wait for the 15 November listing before judging Pangu 2.1. Parameter count, context length and
  open-weights scope were still unpublished.
- Track openJiuwen and openEuler as one runtime story: orchestration above, OS-level isolation below.
- Ask for a reproducible benchmark. Every capability number in this section is vendor-supplied.

## 4. Devices: the agent entry point slipped; driving scaled

**What shipped.** Xiaoyi became a standalone app (11.7.9.300) in the HarmonyOS PC AppGallery on 6
November, and Xiaoyi Work moved from beta to official release with one-line briefs for data analysis,
decks and reports, 200+ experts and DingTalk/Feishu connectors. Pricing is undisclosed.

**What changed.** Huawei confirmed on 13 November that the HarmonyOS 7 Xiaoyi AI quick bar slipped again:
only Mate 90, Mate 90 Pro, Mate 90 RS and Pura X View ship in mid-November; Mate 80, Pura 90, Mate X7
and nova 16 move to December; older flagships to a second December wave; and the feature needs 12GB of
RAM or more *(staged rollout)*. Separately, Huawei said Qiankun ADS now runs in 20+ brands with more
than 2 million vehicles and 5.37 million avoided potential collisions, and the new AITO M8 ships an
L3-oriented four-lidar, 38-sensor suite as standard *(fleet figures vendor-reported)*.

**What to do.**

- Plan the device agent around a staged rollout and a 12GB floor. The install base that gets it is
  smaller than the phone launch suggests.
- Treat the PC app as the real agent surface for now: it updates independently of the phone OS.
- For automotive, separate the shipped ADS 5 fleet numbers from the announced L3-ready hardware.

## 5. Policy: the rules now point at Huawei's own products

**What shipped.** MIIT published the agent-terminal notice on 6 November — dated 24 October, Gong Xin
Ting Xin Guan [2026] No. 42. It requires pre-installed agents on phones, tablets and wearables to pass
agent-adaptation verification and anti-fraud assessment, carry a unique agent identity code, submit OTA
plans for expert review, and obtain app-level e-signed consent before invoking apps. It applies from
issuance *(state media)*. CAICT and industry parties including Huawei released the Agent Development
Self-Discipline Convention at the Wuzhen summit on 4 November, with eight commitments and an agent
identity registration platform *(announced)*.

**What to do.**

- Put the terminal verification and the unique agent ID into the launch critical path for any Huawei
  device sold in China.
- Expect OTA review to slow feature releases for system agents such as Xiaoyi.
- Track the identity registration platform: it is where agent discovery and interoperability will be
  decided.

## 6. Money: procurement, not funding

**What shipped.** China Mobile moved its 2026–27 AI supernode tender — 20,480 cards, 448 RoCE switches,
82 storage systems, RMB 11.6–11.7bn — into contracts on 2–3 November. Dahua's Huaqi Zhihui confirmed
about RMB 2.63bn for 4,096 cards; Digital China's ShenkunTai confirmed RMB 1.317bn for 2,048 cards and
signed a separate RMB 870m Ascend 950DT liquid-cooled SuperPoD order, the first 950DT order through
Huawei's OEM channel *(contracts disclosed, acceptance pending)*. The average of about RMB 570k per card
shows the premium SuperPoDs carry. DeepSeek was reported to plan about 160,000 Ascend 950DT chips for a
1GW Ulanqab site, about $2.56bn, weighted to inference *(reported)*.

**What to do.**

- Treat the China Mobile contracts as the first recurring revenue proof, not the DeepSeek report.
- Verify the DeepSeek order before modelling 2027 capacity; neither company confirmed it.
- Price the integration overhead: the per-card figure includes far more than silicon.

## Decisions for the next 90 days

1. **Model HBM risk in every Ascend 950/960 plan.** Self-sourcing is now a real delivery scenario.
2. **Use AgentArts 4+ as a bid credential**, and ask what exactly was evaluated.
3. **Use the 15 November openPangu-2.1-Pro MaaS listing as the baseline.** It is live at 0.8/3.2 yuan
   per million input/output tokens, text-only, 512K context, Guiyang region only, weights not
   published.
4. **Put MIIT terminal verification and the agent ID in the launch plan.**
5. **Test openJiuwen Hyper-Agent against your own IM and memory model.** It assumes third-party IMs.
6. **Evaluate openEuler Agentic Infra only with a partner distribution**, not as a Huawei-only stack.
7. **Do not equate SuperPoD counts with 950 shipments.**
8. **Watch GITEX (7–11 December) for the enterprise-agent story outside China.**

## What to watch

- **30 November:** openPangu-2.0-Flash and Pro retire; CloudRobo-WM1.2 weights and code are due.
- **30 December:** AgentArts international launch.
- **December:** Xiaoyi quick bar and Work reach older flagships; the 12GB floor decides reach.
- **7–11 December:** GITEX Global, Dubai. Huawei is the only Platinum sponsor, with a 1,225 m² booth in
  Hall S3, Stand E20, themed "Advancing the Agentic World", and SVP Phillip Gan on the Summit stage.
- **HBM:** whether 960DT customers accept self-sourcing, and whether CXMT yield improves.
