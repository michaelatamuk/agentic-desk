# October 2026 — cut log

Working record for the 31 October redo. The brief was rebuilt from a 20–31 October research window and
merged into the 26 October draft, so "cut" here means one of three things: dropped from the canonical
engineering edition, demoted to the actors or China edition, or excluded because it fell outside the
window and was not re-verified.

A cut item is not an unimportant item. Where the reason was space, it says so.

Seven were added to the canonical edition:

  largest miss: the brief documented containment failing but not agents being used offensively at
  machine speed. It is now in the top six and in section 4.
- **Anthropic's self-replication study** (12 October) — a 9B model setting up inference on IoT
  hardware. Added to section 4.
- **Microsoft Guardian Agents** (19 October) — the "separate the supply of intelligence from the
  authority over it" argument. Added to section 6.
- **Thrive Holdings buying ADT** (29 October, $7.6B equity, $15.3B enterprise value) — added to
  section 8, and it displaces Guidewire as the month's clearest AI-buys-non-AI deal.
- **Canada's $1.6B stake in Cohere** (29 October) — added to section 8.
- **Google sunsetting Jules** (29 October) — added to section 1 as consolidation.
- **DoorDash's "several hundred" agent orders** (30 October) — added to section 5 as the counterweight
  to the agentic-commerce story.

Two more were folded in as clauses rather than entries: **China's draft export controls covering
agents** (16 October, section 7) and the **UAE's 50%-of-government plan** (22 October, section 6).

Left out of the canonical edition: **Replit switching its default from Anthropic to OpenAI** (17

already in section 8. **UK agent-incident planning** (12 October) is covered by the DRCF statement
already in the brief.

## 8. Corrections the audit forced

Three factual errors, now fixed in all editions:

1. **Amazon blocked Meta's Muse on 21 September**, not 21 October. The earlier draft had it in the
   wrong month. The Perplexity dispute is older still — a 2025 lawsuit Amazon lost in August — so the
   row conflated two events and a date.
2. **Character.AI's sale to Disney is not complete.** Disney is the opening bidder at $20M for part of
   the IP; the sale had not closed by month end.
3. **"The largest retailer" was wrong.** Amazon is the largest online retailer; Walmart is larger by
   revenue and gated rather than blocked agents. The sentence now names the actual refusers.

The lesson worth keeping: the errors were all in the *framing* sentences, not the sourced claims. The
research had the dates; the compression into a table row and a closing line lost them.
