# Agentic AI in China — November 2026: who did what (as of 14 November)

Every line is one actor and one action, in date order within each section. A claim that is
reported rather than confirmed is marked *(reported)*; state media is labelled.

## The month in six lines

- Five Chinese labs shipped a frontier-class model in eleven days: Tencent Hy4, Moonshot Kimi
  K3.1 Pro, Ant Ling-3.2-flash, Alibaba Qwen4-Max and Baidu ERNIE 5.5 Flash.
- Open weights stayed the default strategy for three of them, while Ant kept its new generation
  API-only.
- Office agents became multi-agent workspaces: Alibaba's Qianwen Office 2.0 put a shared human–agent
  space above DingTalk, Feishu and WeCom, and ByteDance opened Doubao Work to rival models.
- Beijing built the compliance layer for agent hardware and consumer agents: a self-discipline
  convention on 4 November and an MIIT pre-installation approval gate on 6 November.
- WeChat opened a reviewed submission path for third-party agents inside its 1.3bn-user ecosystem.
- DeepSeek turned its model API into an app stack, adding search, OCR, ASR and TTS as metered tools.

## 1. Models and open weights

**Tencent** released **Hy4** (混元 Hy4) on 4 November — a 770B-parameter mixture-of-experts model with
49B active parameters, 1M-token context and vision for the first time. It beat DeepSeek V4.1-Flash on
ProgramBench, FrontierCode2.0 and Agents' Last Exam while trailing GPT-6.1 Sol and Claude Opus 5.5;
open weights were promised "in coming days".

**Moonshot AI** published **Kimi K3.1 Pro** open weights on Hugging Face on 5 November — 2.8T total
parameters, 104B active, native multimodal input, about 1M context, released in MXFP4 with
quantisation-aware training, plus OpenAI- and Anthropic-compatible APIs.

**Ant Group** put **Ling-3.2-flash** (百灵) live on 5 November — 560B total / 25B active, 1M context,
native image, text and video input, and compatible endpoints. It is API-only, a deliberate break
from the open-weights strategy of its peers. Vendor numbers claim wins over Claude Opus 5 on
OmniDocBench 1.5 (92.21 vs 90.56) and Vision2Web (85.86 vs 80.20).

**Zhipu** was reported to be preparing **GLM-5.5** for the week of 9–15 November, after leaked
post-training runs across 32B, 122B and 256B models *(reported)*. Zhipu has not confirmed a date and
its shipping flagship remains GLM-5.3.

**MiniMax** released and open-weighted **H3-Image** on 10 November — a 33B dense diffusion
transformer derived from H3 that generates 4096×4096 images in roughly 10–20 seconds and accepts up
to 16 reference images. Day-zero support came from Ascend, Kunlunxin and Moore Threads, tying the
release to domestic accelerators.

**Alibaba** put **Qwen4-Max Preview** on its Token Plan on 11 November — a multimodal flagship with
1M-token context, text/image/video input, web search, tool calling and structured output. The
preview runs to 25 November and is already wired into the Qianwen app, Qianwen Office, Qoder and
QwenStudio.

**Baidu** launched **ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview** at Baidu World on 12 November — 280B total / 14B
active, 1M context, weights promised open by 25 November — alongside two application models,
ERNIE Lightning and ERNIE Yijing.

**DeepSeek** opened its full app-stack API on 13 November. `deepseek-flash` now supports web search,
deep-thinking search, PDF/Office/image input, link reading and safety moderation across Messages,
Responses and Chat Completions, with standalone Search, Fetch, OCR, ASR and TTS endpoints and an
open-sourced demo client.

## 2. Agent products and surfaces

**Alibaba** launched **Qianwen Office 2.0** (千问办公) on 9 November — a "Team Agent" with a shared
collaboration space, a configurable digital employee ("小Q"), event-triggered cloud tasks, multi-user
and multi-agent co-editing, and handoff across phone, PC and web. It is designed to sit above
DingTalk, Feishu and WeCom as a context layer rather than replace them.

**ByteDance** opened **Doubao Work** (豆包工作) to third-party models on 12 November — Kimi K3.1 Pro,
GLM 5.3 and DeepSeek V4.1 Flash — with per-model quota multipliers and time-window discounts. The
office agent is now a model marketplace competing on routing and unit economics.

**Baidu** launched **词元所**, a model-token marketplace, at Baidu World on 12 November — more than 100
suppliers, 700bn tokens a day, model fingerprinting and provider credit ratings.

**Tencent** shipped Hy4 simultaneously across Yuanbao, CodeBuddy, WorkBuddy, WeCom and ima at
unchanged API pricing, making the model a default domestic agent backbone (4 November).

**WeChat** opened review and submission for a mini-program "AI development mode" inside its 小微
assistant on 13 November, with a 10%→30%→60%→100% grey-release ladder and a 20% problem-rate
threshold. It is the closest thing China has to a consumer agent app store.

**Xiaohongshu** began testing a standalone app for users to build and share AI agents on 13 November.

## 3. Policy, standards and enforcement

**CAICT** and 19 industry parties — including Huawei, Tencent, Ant, Alibaba, ByteDance, OPPO, Honor,
Xiaomi and Baidu — signed the **《智能体发展自律公约》** (Agent Development Self-Discipline Convention) at
the Wuzhen summit on 4 November. Eight commitments cover agent identity, interconnection,
interoperability, safety cut-offs and user rights.

**MIIT** disclosed the **《关于加强智能体终端设备进网管理的通知》** on 6 November — a notice dated 24 October
and effective immediately. It requires agent-adaptation verification, fraud-risk pre-assessment and
expert review of agent OTA upgrades for phones, tablets and wearables with pre-installed agents, plus
distribution-platform disclosure and unique agent ID codes. *(Xinhua / Economic Information Daily,
state media)*

## 4. Hardware and networks

**OPPO** published a terminal–network **agent intent-communication** white paper for 6G on 5 November
(《意图通信》), building on a structured-intent proposal that entered 3GPP TR 23.801-01 and on live-network
agent demos with China Mobile and ZTE. The argument moves from on-device models to carrier standards
where intent, not data, is what the network schedules.

## 5. Money and market

No funding round above $100M, acquisition or IPO was confirmed in the window. The month's commercial
news was product and pricing.

## 6. Incidents and security

No confirmed agent containment or security incident was disclosed by a Chinese lab or regulator in
the window.
