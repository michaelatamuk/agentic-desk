The decision receiptWhat a decision carries

A decision is not a prompt

It is a receipt anyone can inspect.

Situation

What the room believes, with evidence and uncertainty.

Options

Wait, hint, ask a peer, share the board, escalate.

Hard constraints

Consent, privacy, no interruption, cooldown, teacher-only.

Choice

One verb, one action, one score.

Confidence

Where it came from. Calibrated or not.

Approval

Who must agree before anything happens.

Alternatives

What lost, and the real reason it lost.

Record

What happened, who approved, what changed.

For a teacher, this is trust. For a builder, this is the contract. For a manager, this is the audit trail.

LAIX · decision receipt04
This is the memorable artifact. Managers remember “decision receipt”; builders remember “contract.” Say: “A prompt gives you an answer. A decision gives you an answer, the alternatives, the constraints, the approval and the record.”

Manager lensWhy this matters

The buyer does not buy a decision layer

Less noise. Faster help. A record they can defend.

Less interruption

The room waits when evidence is weak. Teachers stop chasing every signal.

Consistent help

Peer help, private hints and escalation follow the same policy, not the loudest voice.

Auditable trust

Every decision shows its evidence, alternatives, approval and outcome.

One decision layer reused across apps is cheaper and safer than a different chatbot in every product.

Manager lens · 20 minutes06
Managers care about teacher time, consistency and risk. Do not sell “AI.” Sell fewer unnecessary interventions, a consistent escalation path, and a record the school can explain to a parent or auditor. The reuse point is the business case: one layer, many hosts.

What is real todayDo not overclaim

Real LAIX. Real records. Simulated room.

Real

Pinned LAIX v0.7.0, adapter, policy plugin, decisions, alternatives, constraints, records, outcomes, audit.

Simulated

Learner inputs, hand raise, speech line, peer offer and disconnect. No raw media leaves the room.

Not yet

Real WebRTC, STT/TTS, PPMA, Digital Mate, JiuwenSwarm, auth, LMS/SIS, billing.

This is enough to prove the decision boundary. It is not enough to claim a classroom outcome.

Truth · real vs simulated12
Be precise. The LAIX dependency and decision records are real. The room inputs are simulated. There is no learning-outcome claim. Builders will trust the boundary more if the limits are explicit.
